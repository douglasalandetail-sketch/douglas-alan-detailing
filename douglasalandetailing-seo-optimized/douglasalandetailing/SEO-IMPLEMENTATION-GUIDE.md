## WHAT WAS OPTIMIZED AND WHY

### 1. Title Tags
Every page now has a unique, keyword-rich title under 60 characters that includes:
- The specific service name
- "Wichita & Manhattan KS" (local signal)
- "Douglas Alan Detailing" (brand)

### 2. Meta Descriptions
Each page has a unique 150–160 character description with:
- A clear value proposition
- Location keywords
- A call-to-action ("Free quote today!")

### 3. Canonical URLs
Prevents duplicate content penalties if your site is ever accessed
via `http://`, `www.`, or trailing-slash variations.

### 4. Open Graph & Twitter Cards
When your pages are shared on Facebook, iMessage, or Twitter,
they now display a proper title, description, and image instead of
blank or garbled previews. Update the `og:image` paths to point
to real photos once you have them.

### 5. LocalBusiness JSON-LD Structured Data (index.html)
This is the single most important SEO change. It tells Google:
- This is a local auto detailing business
- You serve Wichita, KS and Manhattan, KS
- Your hours, services, and contact info
- This can trigger a rich result / Knowledge Panel in Google Search

### 6. Service Schema (service pages)
Each service page declares itself as a specific `Service` entity,
helping Google understand what each page is about and show it for
relevant "near me" searches.

### 7. BreadcrumbList Schema
Helps Google display breadcrumb trails in search results (e.g.,
"Home > Interior Detailing"), improving click-through rates.

### 8. noindex on Non-Content Pages
`thank-you.html` and `privacy-policy.html` are set to `noindex`.
This prevents Google from indexing thin/duplicate content pages
that could dilute your overall site quality score.

### 9. sitemap.xml (rebuilt)
- Only indexes pages that should rank (no thank-you or privacy pages)
- Correct priority weighting (homepage = 1.0, services = 0.9)
- `lastmod` dates set to today

### 10. robots.txt (new file)
- Explicitly blocks crawling of noindex pages
- Points Google to your sitemap
- This file did not previously exist — Google was guessing
